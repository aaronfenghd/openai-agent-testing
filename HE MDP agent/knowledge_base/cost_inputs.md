# Cost Inputs for Oncology CEA

Typical cost categories:
- Drug acquisition
- Administration
- Monitoring and follow-up
- Disease management by health state
- Adverse event management
- Subsequent treatment
- Terminal/end-of-life care

Important assumptions:
- Dosing basis and body-size assumptions
- Wastage assumptions
- Treatment duration and stopping rules
- Relative dose intensity

Questions for the user:
- Which payer perspective and price year should be used?
- Are confidential discounts/rebates relevant?
- Are post-progression costs available by regimen?
