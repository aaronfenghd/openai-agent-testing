# ERG/EAG Critique Themes in Oncology CEA

## Purpose
Considering common ERG/EAG critique themes helps anticipate review risk and strengthen model planning before implementation.

## Frequent critique themes
- Immature OS evidence and uncertainty in long-term extrapolation
- Proportional hazards assumptions not clinically supported
- Inappropriate treatment effect duration or no waning assessment
- Cure assumptions lacking clinical plausibility support
- Post-progression survival assumptions not well justified
- Subsequent treatment assumptions not representative
- Utility sources with transferability concerns
- Omitted or weakly justified cost categories
- Adverse event incidence/duration assumptions not transparent
- Comparator selection concerns
- Subgroup/generalizability concerns
- Insufficient scenario analysis coverage

## How the HE MDP Agent should use this guidance
- Use critique themes as a prompt framework, not as proof of project-specific issues.
- Flag where the current plan may be vulnerable and ask targeted clarification questions.
- Recommend scenario and validation steps where critique risk is high.

## Common HTA implications
- Models may require broader scenario sets if survival and treatment-effect assumptions are uncertain.
- Comparator and subgroup decisions require explicit clinical and policy rationale.
- Utility and cost assumptions require transparent source justification and transferability assessment.

## Questions the HE MDP Agent should ask
- Which critique themes are most likely for this indication and setting?
- Are comparator choices and subgroup definitions defensible?
- Which assumptions need explicit challenge scenarios?
- What evidence is available to reduce key review risks?

## MDP implications
The MDP should include a reviewer-risk section mapping key assumptions to planned mitigation (data requests, scenarios, validation checks, and open questions).

## Example wording
"The plan will proactively address likely review concerns, including extrapolation uncertainty, treatment-effect duration, subsequent therapy assumptions, and utility/cost source validity. Targeted scenarios and validation checks will be prespecified to reduce decision uncertainty."
