# Model Assumptions in Oncology CEA

Key assumptions to evaluate:
- Proportional hazards assumption
- Constant hazard ratio after a specific time point
- Treatment effect waning
- Cure assumption
- Mixture cure assumptions
- Mortality constraints vs general population mortality
- Treatment duration assumptions
- Subsequent therapy assumptions
- Adverse event timing and duration assumptions

Questions to ask the user:
- Which assumptions are evidence-based vs structural placeholders?
- Which assumptions are most decision-sensitive?
- What assumption scenarios are expected by reviewers?
