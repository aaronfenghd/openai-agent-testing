# Survival Extrapolation Guidance

Core considerations:
- OS, PFS, and TTD alignment
- Independent vs dependent curve fitting
- PH-based vs independently fitted treatment effects

Modeling approaches:
- Standard parametric distributions
- Flexible spline models
- Cure and mixture-cure models

Validation considerations:
- External validation against long-term evidence or registries
- Clinical expert validation of hazards/tails
- Constraints using general population mortality

Scenario examples:
- Alternative parametric families
- Alternative treatment waning onset/intensity
- Cure fraction vs non-cure structures
